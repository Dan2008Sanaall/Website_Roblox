    Privacy Policy

    How BloxBeacon handles visitor information.

    Starter text

    This policy describes the site as it is currently built. Review it, and have it checked against the laws that apply to you, before launching or adding analytics, adverts or forms.

    What this site collects

    As built, BloxBeacon is a static website. It has no accounts, no contact form, no analytics scripts, no advertising scripts and sets no cookies.

    Search and copy buttons

    Search runs entirely in your browser using a small index file. What you type is not sent to us. Copy buttons place text on your clipboard and do nothing else.

    Hosting

    Whichever company hosts the site may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and requested page, under its own privacy policy.

    Children

    We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

    Changes

    If the site starts collecting any data, this page will be updated to explain what and why.
